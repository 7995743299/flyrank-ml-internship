# Study Coach AI

## Overview

Study Coach AI is an AI-powered learning assistant developed during the FlyRank AI Fluency Internship.

The project helps students generate structured study plans, answer learning-related questions, and receive guidance for effective learning through conversational AI.

---

## Who It Is For

Study Coach AI is designed for students and learners who want help organizing their learning, creating study plans, and getting learning guidance.

---

## Features

- Generate personalized study plans
- Answer learning-related questions
- Provide AI-powered learning recommendations
- Help students organize their learning journey
- Provide structured step-by-step learning guidance
- Support conversational learning
